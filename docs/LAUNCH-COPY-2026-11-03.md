- One log for weighted basics **and** skills
- Real progress, not fake streaks: sets, reps, load, PRs
- Skill ladders with visible next steps
- Local-first, optional sync, no analytics
- Native desktop app + installable PWA

## FAQ

**Is it free?**  
The core app is free and local-first. Paid plans are for the hosted/sync tier; pricing is shown in the app.

**Does it work offline?**  
Yes. The app is local-first; sync is optional.

**Do you sell my data?**  
No. There is no analytics/ad tracking; see the privacy page.

**Is this medical advice?**  
No. It is a training log. Warm up, progress gradually, and stop if something hurts.
