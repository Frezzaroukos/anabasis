Why Anabasis

Most logs count sets. You are building a ladder.

When the goal is a front lever, a planche or a muscle-up, reps are only one number. The real question is: which rung comes next?

Weighted basics + skills, in one place.

Pull-ups with load, dips, pistols — and the skill progressions they feed. No second app, no spreadsheet, no translation layer.

The next rung is always visible.

Locked steps stay on screen. Progress is a path, not a guess — and every session has a direction.

A log built for the long game.

Offline-first. No ads. No analytics. Everything lives on your device by default; an optional account only exists if you want sync. Your training is not a feed.

Questions

Straight answers, no funnel.

The short version: the log is free, your data is yours, and the founding price is real until the counter runs out.

Still unsure? Read the safety notes or browse the source. The app speaks for itself.

Is Anabasis free?

Yes. The core training log — workouts, calendar, skill ladders and goals — is free, offline and private. Pro adds Coach mode, deload detection and unlimited programs and goals.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works locally with no signup. An account is optional and only adds cross-device sync and social features.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The full log is offline-first; sync waits until you are back online. Install it as a PWA for the full-screen app feel.

What is the founding price?

The first 250 lifetime members pay €49.99 once instead of €99.99. The counter reads the live remaining spots from checkout; when it reaches zero, founding closes.

Where does my data live?

On your device by default. No ads, no analytics, no trackers. Export your data whenever you want.

Which platforms are supported?

Any modern browser, installable as a PWA on iOS and Android, plus a native desktop build.

Can I bring my history?

Yes. Import a previous Anabasis export and keep the years you already put in.
