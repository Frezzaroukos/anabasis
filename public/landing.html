Why Anabasis

Most logs count sets. You are building a ladder.

When the goal is a front lever, a planche or a muscle-up, reps are only one number. The real question is: which rung comes next?

Weighted basics + skills, in one place.

Pull-ups with load, dips, pistols — and the skill progressions they feed. No second app, no spreadsheet, no translation layer.

The next rung is always visible.

Locked steps stay on screen. Progress is a path, not a guess — and every session has a direction.

A log built for the long game.

Offline-first. No ads. No analytics. Everything lives on your device by default; an optional account only exists if you want sync. Your training is not a feed.

Inside the app

A training log, not a dashboard.

Calendar-first, set-by-set, with skill ladders and goals that speak your language. Open it, log the work, see the next step.

Real app screens from a demo profile. No invented metrics.
