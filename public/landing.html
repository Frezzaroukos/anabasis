Free · open source · works on phone, tablet and desktop.

What it solves

The only two things that matter for this kind of training.

Weighted basics + skills, together

Log load and reps for your pull-ups, dips and pistols, and track where you are on each skill — in one place.

See the next rung

Every skill is a ladder. Anabasis shows the step you're on and what comes next.

Yours, offline, private

Everything works offline in your browser. No ads, no analytics, no trackers.

A look inside

Calendar-centric, built for the climb.

For whom

For people who train weighted calisthenics and skills.

Weighted pull-ups, dips and pistols, and skills like the front lever, planche and muscle-up.

Install it

  1. iPhone (Safari): open the app, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  2. Android (Chrome): open the app, menu, then Install app.
  3. It installs like a native app and works fully offline. A Linux desktop build is also available.
